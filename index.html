Wing Commander Brief · Strategic Posture

Speed of Relevance

Rainier Spark is delivering measurable lift across all four 62d AW priorities. FY26 baseline returns — 11,400 Airman hours recovered and verified work-center savings — fund the next wave of autonomous inspection, human-performance, and 3D-printing capability.

FY27 projections are calculated by scaling the current 11,400 recovered Airman hours and applying an optimized maintenance-hour cost baseline ($165/hr blended rate) across expanded autonomous-inspection cycles (×3 frequency) and 3D-printing production runs (Prusa XL throughput + 627 CES line). This yields ~$2.6M FY27 cost savings and ~16.2K Airman hours returned.

The CPE-CBRND MOU positions the wing as the AMC pathfinder for biometric-enabled crew-rest decisions, while sUAS-enabled C-17 inspections compress the pre-mission cycle and free maintainers for higher-priority sorties.
